## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an airy, sun-drenched, radiant editorial aesthetic rooted in contemporary fashion publications and luxury lifestyle curation. Moving away from heavy charcoal containers and dense framing, the visual language prioritizes luminous negative space, delicate tonal boundaries, and natural light.

### Personality & Tone
- **Luminous & Sunlit:** Bright, weightless, and open. The canvas breathes with soft warmth rather than sterile clinical white.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-fashion typographic rigor paired with generous margins, evoking the tactile luxury of heavyweight uncoated art paper.
- **Effortless Elegance:** Restrained and refined; information is structured through tonal warmth, subtle hairline dividers, and meticulous typography rather than structural mass.

### Visual Style
The design style is **Radiant Editorial Minimalism**. High-contrast, classic editorial serif headlines pair with an architectural sans-serif workhorse. Dense dark surfaces are eliminated in favor of luminous ivory, warm alabaster, sun-washed sand, and refined warm taupe accents. Surfaces feel illuminated from within, using paper-like micro-tonalities to establish depth and structure.

## Colors

The palette is tuned for high-key radiance, replacing oppressive dark volumes with delicate ivory, fresh beige, and luminous sand. High-contrast ink accents preserve editorial legibility without overpowering the sunlit atmosphere.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#24211E` - Refined Soft Charcoal):** An espresso-tinged charcoal used deliberately for typographic clarity, primary interactive buttons, and razor-sharp iconography.
- **Secondary (`#A47E5B` - Luminous Sand Ochre):** A warm, golden camel accent for focal highlights, curated badges, and stylist selection states.
- **Tertiary (`#7B806E` - Sunlit Sage):** A botanical, sun-washed neutral accent applied to composition metadata, sustainable material callouts, and secondary tags.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF9F6` - Luminous Soft Ivory):** The core atmospheric foundation. It provides radiant, soft warmth that avoids the eye fatigue of stark, uncalibrated white.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF9F6` (Luminous Ivory)
- **Elevated Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Luminous White for crisp, light-reflective card pop)
- **Nested / Inset Surface:** `#F4F1EA` (Fresh Beige for soft internal grouping)
- **Highlight Tint:** `#EDE7DE` (Luminous Sand for active filters and muted focal states)
- **Hairline Dividers:** `#E8E3DA` applied at 1px thickness to emulate magazine page rules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Bodoni Moda** for high-fashion posture with **Manrope** for architectural modernism and crystalline readability.

- **Bodoni Moda:** Drives the editorial masthead, collection titles, and issue numbers. It commands the page with dramatic thick-thin strokes and timeless haute-couture poise.
- **Manrope:** Chosen for all functional body and metadata copy. With open counters and generous spacing, it ensures reading comfort amidst airy whitespace.
- **Labels & Categorization:** All `label-*` styles enforce uppercase transformation and expanded letter-tracking (`0.09em` to `0.12em`), providing refined taxonomy tags such as `COLLECTION N° 08`, `PURE LINEN`, and `LOOKBOOK EDIT`.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are treated like open editorial spreads, favoring wide margins and unhurried whitespace over dense component stacking.

### Grid & Margins
- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.5rem` (`margin`) outer edge breathing room and `1.25rem` gutters. Elements flow in a single, unencumbered column.
- **Tablet (640px–1024px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` outer margin and `1.75rem` gutters. Supports asymmetric pairs and side-by-side editorial critiques.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px` centered within a `4rem` outer canvas frame, creating a curated gallery view.

### Rhythmic Breathing Room
- Structural sections (e.g., lookbook carousels, editor's notes) must be separated by `space-xl` (`3rem`) to create natural visual pauses.
- Related groupings within cards or panels adhere strictly to `space-md` (`1rem`) and `space-lg` (`1.75rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through high-key ambient illumination and tonal layering rather than dense artificial drop shadows. Surfaces feel like stacked fine-art paper under natural gallery light.

### Elevation Principles
- **Base Canvas:** `#FAF9F6` creates a soft, luminous undertone across all viewports.
- **Layered Elements (Cards & Panels):** Lifted via clean `#FFFFFF` fills outlined with a subtle `1px solid #E8E3DA` perimeter hairline.
- **Floating Overlays & Docks:** Suspended components (such as floating actions or navigation bars) use an ultra-diffused, sunlit ambient shadow: `0 12px 36px rgba(36, 33, 30, 0.04), 0 2px 8px rgba(36, 33, 30, 0.02)` atop a luminous semi-transparent backing (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `8px` backdrop blur).
- **Modals & Drawers:** Layered over a soft warm-taupe veil (`rgba(36, 33, 30, 0.25)` with `6px` blur) to preserve the ambient radiance of the underlying screen.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a soft, precisely tailored geometry (`roundedness: 1`), evoking high-end paper stationery, linen book covers, and structured fashion cuts.

- **Cards & Outer Containers:** Soft `0.5rem` corners maintain a crisp silhouette without feeling cold.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs):** Subtle `0.25rem` (`rounded-sm`) to `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`) corner radii deliver tailored architectural structure.
- **Tags & Status Pills:** Full oval pills (`9999px`) provide a gentle organic contrast against rectangular editorial photography.
- **Media Containers:** Fashion imagery adheres to `0.5rem` corner radii with classical portrait aspect ratios (`3:4` or `4:5`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#24211E` fill with `#FAF9F6` text. Height `46px`, font `title-md`, radius `0.25rem`. Hover triggers a soft taupe lighten (`#3D3834`).
- **Secondary / Luminous Action:** Fresh Beige `#F4F1EA` fill with `#24211E` text and `1px solid #E8E3DA` border. Clean, radiant, and weightless.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with `#24211E` text and an animated underline offset by `4px`.
- **Editorial Accent Action:** Sand Ochre `#A47E5B` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, reserved for exclusive conversion triggers (e.g., "Reserve Look", "Curate Capsule").

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Geometry: Fully rounded pill (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Default:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E8E3DA` border, `#8C827A` warm taupe text, uppercase `label-md`.
- **Active / Selected:** Luminous Sand `#EDE7DE` background, `1px solid #A47E5B` border, `#24211E` text.
- **Botanical / Material Chip:** Light Sunlit Sage tint (`rgba(123, 128, 110, 0.12)`) with `#5E6453` text for fabric metadata (`"MULBERRY SILK"`, `"RAW CASHMERE"`).

### Cards
- **Product & Moodboard Cards:** Luminous white `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E8E3DA` hairline border and `0.5rem` radius. Padding set to `space-md` (`1rem`).
- Typography inside cards relies on `headline-md` for collection titles paired with subtle numeric tags (`label-sm`).
- Never use heavy drop shadows on resting cards; contrast is produced purely by the `#FFFFFF` surface resting on the `#FAF9F6` ivory background.

### Input Fields
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E8E3DA` border, radius `0.25rem`, height `48px`, internal padding `0 1rem`.
- Focus State: Smooth transition to `1px solid #24211E` border with no outer glow rings.
- Typography: `body-md` in `#24211E` with placeholder styled in warm taupe `#8C827A`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Compact `18px x 18px` frame with `1px solid #E8E3DA` border on `#FFFFFF` ground.
- Selected state fills with `#24211E`, displaying a clean `#FAF9F6` indicator. Checkbox radius `2px`; Radio radius `50%`.

### Editorial-Specific Components
- **Curator’s Aside:** Fresh Beige `#F4F1EA` container with a fine `1px solid #E8E3DA` border and a delicate `2px solid #A47E5B` top accent line. Uses `body-md` italicized prose for stylist commentary.
- **Floating Look Dock:** Bottom-anchored glass dock in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `1px solid #E8E3DA` and ambient diffusion (`0 12px 36px rgba(36, 33, 30, 0.04)`), grouping capsule assembly controls.
- **Editorial Pull Quote:** Large Bodoni Moda display quote centered with top and bottom hairline borders (`1px solid #E8E3DA`) and ample `space-lg` vertical breathing space.
